Our mission is to share the thrill of the river safely, with certified guides and unforgettable memories on every trip.

History

Wild River Rafting began in 2010 with two guides, one raft, and a love for the river. Today we run trips for families, friends, and adventure seekers on some of the best rapids in the region.
